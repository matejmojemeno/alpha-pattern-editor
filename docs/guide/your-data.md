# Your data

The app has no accounts and no server that stores anything. Your projects, and the
pictures you import, stay on your device, in the browser you use. That keeps them private,
and it means keeping them safe is up to you.

## Where your projects are kept

Projects are saved in this browser's storage for the app's site, on this device only.
Every change is saved as you make it. Your [Settings](settings.md), and what you enter in
[Yarn & size](yarn-and-size.md), are kept in the browser too.

After you save your first project, the app asks the browser to keep its storage rather
than clear it when space runs low. Browsers decide for themselves whether to agree.

## What leaves your device

Nothing you make or import is uploaded:

- **Photos and screenshots** are read on your device. The chart reader runs inside your
  browser.
- **Patterns and progress** are saved in your browser and nowhere else.

The only download is the chart reader itself, about 9 MB, fetched from the app's own site
the first time you import a picture, and kept by your browser after that. Opening,
designing and following patterns never needs it.

**Visits are counted**, and nothing else. When you open the app, the page tells Cloudflare
Web Analytics about the visit. It sets no cookies and keeps no ID for you. It sends the
page's address without the part after `#`, so never which project you have open; the site
you came from; which browser and system you use; and how fast the page loaded. It can't
tell whether two visits came from the same person, and it never sees your projects or
pictures. Ad blockers often stop it, and the app works the same either way.

**Feedback**, on the home screen, starts an email in your own mail app, with the app's
version filled in. Nothing is sent until you send it, and it goes only to us. (Issues on
GitHub, the other way to report a problem, are public.)

## Clearing browser data deletes your projects

Clearing your browser's data for the site ("cookies and site data", or "website data")
deletes every project in it. Browsers can also clear it on their own, for example after a
long time without a visit, and private or incognito windows forget everything when you
close them. Nobody can get the projects back afterwards, because no copy exists
anywhere else.

So [export](library.md#export-a-project) anything you'd hate to lose, and keep the
`.alpha` files somewhere safe.

## Each browser, device and web address has its own library

Projects saved in one browser don't show in another, even on the same computer, and don't
follow you to your phone. The same goes for the app at a different web address: each
address has its own, separate library. To move a project, export it and import it there
([Move a project to another device](library.md#move-a-project-to-another-device-or-browser)).
