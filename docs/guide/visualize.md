# Visualize

**Visualize**, in the Design stage's top bar, draws the pattern as crocheted fabric, in
the stitch you choose. A chart's squares are all the same size, but stitches aren't
square, so the finished piece is often shorter or taller than the chart suggests.
Visualize shows you that before you start.

<img src="media/design-visualize.png" alt="Visualize: the pattern drawn as single crochet stitches, with the stitch, row direction and proportions to choose" width="600">

## Choose the stitch

**Stitch** offers:

- **Single crochet**: the usual stitch for alpha charts, small and dense, a little wider
  than tall.
- **Single crochet, back loop only** and **Single crochet, front loop only**: the loop
  you don't work shows as a ridge in that row's colour.
- **Half double crochet**: taller than wide.
- **Double crochet**: about twice as tall as wide, with gaps between the stitches where
  carried yarn shows through.
- **Waistcoat (knit) stitch**: dense Vs that look like knitting.
- **Corner to corner (C2C)**: each square of the chart is a small square tile, worked in
  diagonal rows.

## Choose how the rows are worked

Under **Rows**:

- **Turned at each end**: you turn the work at the end of every row, so every other row
  shows the back of its stitches.
- **Right side always facing**: every row is worked from the same side, as in the round
  or when you cut the yarn at each end.

C2C is always turned, so this choice is off for it.

**Show carried yarn** draws the colours you carry inside the stitches, as the Work
stage's [carrying hints](work.md#show-where-to-carry-yarn) suggest. In tall stitches it
shows through the gaps. It isn't offered for C2C.

## Choose the proportions

Under **Proportions**:

- **Typical** uses published gauges for that stitch. Below the picture, the dialog says
  what that does to the shape, such as "Stitches are 80% as tall as they are wide, so the
  picture comes out 20% shorter than the chart."
- **My swatch** uses your own swatch from [Yarn & size](yarn-and-size.md#measure-a-swatch).
  Enter its width and height there first.

Zoom with the **−**, **Fit** and **+** buttons, by scrolling, or by pinching, and drag to
move around.

## Why it isn't exact

Visualize is a guide to the shape and the look, not a photograph:

- The typical proportions come from published gauges, which are one yarn, hook and
  crocheter each. Your tension changes them; **My swatch** is closer.
- Back and front loop only use single crochet's proportions: no measured figure for them
  was found.
- The stitches are drawn from how each one is made, not photographed, and real yarn
  varies in fuzz, sheen and colour.

The gauges it uses, and where each comes from, are listed in
[the stitch sources](../../web/src/stitch/README.md).
